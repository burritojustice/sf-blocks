    The Spelunker can't be read from here
    
      
    The Spelunker doesn't let other websites read its lists of descendants, so this page needs a helper in between. Two ways:
    
      
      
        
    • On your computer: in a copy of sf-blocks, run
      python3 wof-descender/descender.py --serve
      and open localhost:8765/wof-descender/.
      • 
        
    • Here: put the address of a proxy (see wof-descender/proxy/) in descender.json, or add ?spelunker=<its address> to this page's link.
      • 
      
    
      
    Or run the script on its own; it writes the same files: python3 wof-descender/descender.py 85922583 -p neighbourhood
